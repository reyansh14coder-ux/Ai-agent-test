    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is it actually autonomous?

    Yes — agents wake on schedule, read live company data, and act with real tools. The one exception is deliberate: anything that goes outward (emails, invoices, posts) queues for your approval. Autonomy where it saves time, you where it matters.

    What does it connect to?

    Out of the box it runs a complete in-app company database — CRM, support tickets, invoices, content, tasks, calendar — and agents act on it through a tool layer (MCP). Adapters for email and calendar providers plug into the same approval queue as they're enabled.

    Do I need to know how to code?

    No. Setup is a form: your company, your goals, your schedule. If you can write an email, you can run SoloOS.

    Which AI model does it use?

    It runs on the model stack you already have — bring your key, pick your provider, swap anytime. There is also a built-in mock runtime for demos and testing that needs no key at all.

    What happens while I'm asleep?

    Agents run on their schedules, draft everything, and file it for the morning: replies drafted, reminders ready, content queued. You start the day approving, not catching up.
